# Third-party notices

## UIBVFEDPlus-Light / UIBVFED Physical States

The public preview contains mapped virtual facial-expression images sourced from UIBVFED materials and marked in the project manifest as **CC BY 4.0**. Retain attribution and the original licence notice when redistributing those assets. Provenance is recorded in `stimuli/emotion/LICENSES.md`, `stimulus_sources.csv`, and `manifest.csv`.

## Excluded datasets

KDEF, FACES, NimStim, CFD, CK+, JAFFE, IAPS, and other restricted or unverified collections are not included. Project-generated synthetic images are included only for DEMO/PILOT software testing and are labelled **NOT_FOR_REAL_PARTICIPANTS**.
